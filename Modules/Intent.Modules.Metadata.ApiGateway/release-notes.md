### Version 1.0.0-beta.7

- New feature: **Manage Gateway Routes...** on a gateway's Services package opens a form listing the HTTP endpoints (Commands, Queries and Operations with **Http Settings**) in the packages the gateway references. Tick an endpoint, or a folder for all of its endpoints, to add its route; untick to remove it. Routes whose verb or route no longer match their endpoint are flagged and can be synced. Changes are listed for confirmation before they are applied, and Ctrl+Z undoes them.
